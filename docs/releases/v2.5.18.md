# Morphly v2.5.18

Translation prepares a replacement WebSocket before the Vercel session deadline and on Gemini's shutdown notice. The existing connection continues streaming until replacement setup completes. Billing then settles before the new connection activates, while the local voice engine and playback devices remain running.

Unexpected disconnects retry automatically, including a previous account session still closing. Recovery retains up to two seconds of unsent microphone audio per direction and replays it at a bounded rate. Audio older than that limit is dropped. Stop cancels retries, closes active and standby sockets, and clears the queue. Authentication or insufficient-credit errors still stop translation.

Recurring credit checks no longer block PCM forwarding while existing authorization is valid. Initial authorization uses a separate control exchange; all audio remains subject to the cached credit deadline. Rates remain 2.5 credits/s for translation and 4 credits/s total with face streaming. No new billing migration is required.

Billing network requests have a four-second timeout. Temporary service failures recover automatically, but no audio is forwarded beyond the last authorized deadline. Settlement correctly awaits Supabase's query builder before handling errors. Production logs from the previous release showed billing calls taking 45 seconds and 232 seconds; the cause of those database stalls still requires investigation with access to the production Supabase project.

Validation covers planned rotation, provider shutdown, failed standby preparation, Stop during handoff, recovery buffering, expiration and overflow, billing settlement order, credit expiry, and continued local voice-engine operation. Production speech latency and audible continuity still need a microphone test on the target machine. Fresh Gemini sessions may interrupt a sentence; neither instant responses nor lossless recovery are guaranteed.
