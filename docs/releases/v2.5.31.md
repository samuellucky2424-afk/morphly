# Morphly v2.5.31

- New voice changer powered by ElevenLabs: choose a voice from the dropdown or upload your own voice recording. The local voice engine download is no longer needed.
- Real-time translation has been removed.
- VB-CABLE now installs from the voice panel: select Install Cable, approve the Windows administrator prompt, and the official VB-CABLE installer completes the setup.
- Plus retries its video connection instead of stopping on a temporary video subscription error.
